Function Sense · 21

Composition chains two mappings: every input x travels to f(x) on the middle line, and then on to g(f(x)) on the bottom line. The notation g circle f is read right to left, because the machine written next to x acts first, just like in g(f(x)). The animation shows three chains. Add 1 and then double sends x to 2(x + 1). Swapping the order, double and then add 1, sends x to 2x + 1, a different function, so composition is not commutative. Finally, 2 to the x followed by log base 2: the points spread out on the middle line and then return exactly to where they began. A function followed by its inverse is the identity map.
