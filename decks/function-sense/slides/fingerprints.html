When you meet a new formula or graph, ask the two questions on the left. The answer tells you the family, and the family tells you the shape.

Function Sense · 12

Here is the whole of Part Two in one grid. Rows: what you do to the input, add or multiply. Columns: what the output does, add or multiply. Lines are add-to-add, so they have a constant slope. Exponentials are add-to-multiply, so they explode. Logs are multiply-to-add, the reverse of exponentials, so they crawl. Powers are multiply-to-multiply, so on a log–log plot they become straight lines. The examples on each card are everyday instances of the same fingerprint. The decibel example: ten times the sound power is +10 decibels, a logarithmic scale.
