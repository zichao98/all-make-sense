The one question to ask

Don’t memorize the shape.
Ask what happens when x changes.

Add 1 to x? Double x? Every common function answers in its own steady way. That answer is its fingerprint, and the shape follows from it.

This is the turning point of the deck. Most people try to remember what each graph looks like. That rarely sticks. A better habit: for any function, ask two questions. If I add something to x, what happens to the output? If I multiply x by something, what happens to the output? Lines, powers, exponentials and logs each give a different, very steady answer. Once you know the answer, you can redraw the shape yourself.
