Part Two

Four families

Each one has a fingerprint: which input change gives a steady output change.

Lines turn adding into adding. Powers turn multiplying into multiplying. Exponentials turn adding into multiplying. Logs turn multiplying back into adding.

Almost every function you meet early on is built from four families: lines, powers, exponentials and logarithms. Instead of their shapes, learn their fingerprints. Each one turns a steady change in the input into a steady change in the output, but the kind of change differs: adding or multiplying. The next four slides take them one at a time, then we put all four in one table.
