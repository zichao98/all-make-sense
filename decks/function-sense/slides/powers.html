05 · Powers

Powers: double x, multiply by a fixed factor

Double the input and the output is multiplied by the same factor, wherever you start.

n = 1/2 (root)

×1.41

n = 1

×2

n = 2 (square)

×4: double the side, 4× the area

n = 3 (cube)

×8: double the side, 8× the volume

All of them pass through (1, 1). Below 1 a higher power is smaller; above 1 it is bigger.

Function Sense · 08

Powers of x have a multiplicative fingerprint. If you double x, x to the n gets multiplied by 2 to the n, every time, no matter where you start: from 1 to 2, or from 5 to 10. The square root gives ×1.41, a line ×2, a square ×4, a cube ×8. That is why doubling the side of a square gives four times the area. The animation highlights each power in turn: the black dot at (1, 1) is shared by all of them, and the orange dot at x = 2 sits at 2 to the n. Below 1, raising to a higher power makes numbers smaller, so higher powers hug the floor there and then shoot up after 1.
