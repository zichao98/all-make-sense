Eye estimate: exp beats power beats log

That is a statement about “eventually”. Over a short range, check the numbers.

Function Sense · 13　　Thumbnails: each curve from 0 to 100 on its own vertical scale.

Who grows fastest? At x = 100, log base 10 of x is just 2, x squared is 10,000, and 2 to the 100 is about 1.27 times 10 to the 30, a 31-digit number. The thumbnails each show one curve from 0 to 100 scaled to its own height, so you see the characteristic shape: log rises and then crawls, x squared bends smoothly, and 2 to the x stays flat and then becomes a wall. The surprise card: x to the 10 is actually larger than 2 to the x for every x between about 1.08 and 58.8 (I solved 10 ln x = x ln 2 numerically). Only after 58.8 does the exponential win, and then it wins forever. So the rule exp beats power beats log is true, but only eventually.
