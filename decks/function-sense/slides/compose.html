Part Four

Chaining mappings

Composition: one machine’s output becomes the next one’s input.

Order matters, fingerprints chain together, matrix multiplication is composition, and up close the stretch factors simply multiply.

Once functions are machines, the obvious next move is to plug one machine into another: take the output of f and feed it to g. That is called composition, written g circle f. Almost every function you meet is a chain of simple pieces: 2 to the (3x plus 1) is a line followed by an exponential; a neural network is a long chain of layers. This part shows four things about chains: the order matters, the fingerprints of the pieces combine predictably, multiplying matrices is exactly composing linear maps, and when you zoom in, the local stretch factors multiply, which is the chain rule.
