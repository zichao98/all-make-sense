Function Sense · 09

An exponential turns adding into multiplying. Each time x goes up by 1, 2 to the x doubles. Ten steps give 1,024; twenty give over a million. The animation adds one bar per step and keeps rescaling the vertical axis so the newest bar fits; watch the older bars shrink into nothing. The blue dashed bar is all earlier bars stacked together: it is always exactly one short of the newest bar, because 1 + 2 + 4 + … + 2 to the n−1 equals 2 to the n minus 1. The paper example: 0.1 mm doubled 42 times is 0.1 mm times about 4.4 trillion, roughly 440,000 km, more than the average Earth–Moon distance of 384,400 km. 41 folds would give only about 220,000 km. Physically you cannot fold paper that many times; it is a thought experiment.
