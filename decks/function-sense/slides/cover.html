Function Sense

Stop memorizing shapes. Learn what a function does when x changes.

Mapping · Lines · Powers · Exponentials · Logs · Matrices

Three questions drive this deck. What is a function, really, and what does “mapping” mean? How can I look at x squared, 2 to the x, or log x and immediately feel what it does? And how does the linear-algebra idea of a matrix as a mapping connect to the functions from school? The animation is the picture we will keep using: dots on an input line flow to their places on an output line. Each family of functions sends them in its own recognizable pattern.
