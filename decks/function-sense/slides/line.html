03 · The straight line

ax + b: stretch by a, then slide by b

Stretch: every gap on the line is multiplied by the size of a

Flip: a negative a turns the line around

Slide: then everything shifts by b

Exchange rate

The same exchange rate everywhere: that is why the graph is straight. The slope is a; the height at x = 0 is b.

Function Sense · 05

A straight-line function does two things to the number line. First it stretches it by the factor a: every gap, everywhere, gets multiplied by the same amount. If a is negative, the line also flips. Then it slides everything by b. The orange bar shows the interval from 0 to 1; after the map its length is exactly the size of a. Because the stretch is the same everywhere, adding 1 to x always adds a to the output, no matter where you start. That constant exchange rate is what the slope means, and it is why the graph is straight.
