Part One

One variable

How is it spread out?

Histograms show the shape, box plots summarize it, and a log axis tames skew. Each one can hide something.

Part One is about a single column of numbers. The question is always the same: what does the spread look like? Where is the middle, how wide is it, is there more than one hump, are there extreme values, is it skewed? Three tools answer it: the histogram, the box plot and the log axis.
