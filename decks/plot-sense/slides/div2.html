Part Two

Comparing groups

Which is bigger, and by how much?

Bars, pies and error bars: what each encodes, and the three ways they most often mislead.

Part Two is about comparing categories or groups. Bars are the workhorse, but their honesty depends on the baseline. Pies trade precision for a sense of the whole. And a comparison of averages without any sign of spread can make a noise difference look like a real one.
