How to read, choose and trust a chart

Plot Sense

Every chart answers one question. Pick the question first.

Histogram · Box plot · Bar · Scatter · Heatmap · Line · Log axis

This deck is about one skill: looking at data and knowing which picture will answer your question, and what that picture can hide. The animation is the whole idea in miniature. The same 240 numbers are shown four ways. As raw dots they look like a smear. Stacked into columns they become a dot plot, which is a histogram made of dots, and two humps appear. As bars it is an ordinary histogram. As a box plot it shrinks to five numbers, and the two humps vanish. None of these is wrong. Each answers a different question, and each throws something away. Every chart and number in the deck is computed live in the browser or was checked with a script.
