13 · Smoothing

A moving average trades noise for lag

Daily data mix a weekly cycle, noise and a slow trend. Averaging over a window removes everything faster than the window.

Trailing averages run late: 3 days for a 7-day window, 45 days for a 90-day one.

Eye rule: window = the cycle you want to erase

7 days kills the weekly pattern. Longer windows hide turning points.

Plot Sense · 19

Raw daily data are a mixture: a weekly cycle, here low weekends, random day-to-day noise, and the slow trend you actually care about. A moving average replaces each day with the average of the last w days, which cancels anything that repeats faster than the window. The animation shows one simulated year. Without smoothing, the weekly sawtooth dominates. A 7-day window exactly cancels the weekly cycle. A 30-day window reveals the seasonal rise and fall. A 90-day window is very smooth but late: a trailing average is centered (w − 1)/2 days in the past, so the 90-day line lags turning points by about 45 days and the 7-day line by 3. Choose the window to match the cycle you want to remove, and remember that every bit of smoothness costs some delay.
