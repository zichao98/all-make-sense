Plot Sense · 22

This is the whole deck as one lookup table. Start from the question in the left column, use the chart in the middle, and check the trap on the right before you show it to anyone. For one variable, histogram or box plot with dots, watching the bin width. For skewed data, a log axis and the median. For comparing categories, sorted bars that start at zero, or a dot plot if you need to zoom. For part-to-whole, a stacked bar, with pies only for two or three parts. For comparing groups, points with a labelled interval. For relationships, a scatter plot, switching to density when the points pile up. For time, a line with an honest smoothing window, a log axis for growth, and never two y-axes.
