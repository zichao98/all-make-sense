Plot Sense · 15

The correlation coefficient r measures how tightly the points in a scatter plot cluster around a straight line, from −1 for a perfect falling line through 0 to +1 for a perfect rising line. The animation sweeps through r = 0, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8, 0.95 and −0.7 on 150 points and shows the measured r and r squared. The surprise for most people is how weak moderate correlations look: at r = 0.3 the cloud is almost round. r squared is the share of the variance of y that a straight line in x explains, so r = 0.5 explains only 25 percent and r = 0.9 explains 81 percent. Two warnings: r only measures straight-line association, so a perfect curve can have a low r, as Anscombe's second dataset showed, and correlation alone never establishes cause.
