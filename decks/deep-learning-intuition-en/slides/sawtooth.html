Deep Learning Intuition · 11

This is the classic depth example: the sawtooth function used by Telgarsky in 2016 to show the advantage of deep networks. g stretches [0,1] by a factor of two and folds it back. Applying g again folds every existing segment once more. L layers use just 2L neurons to produce 2^L segments. With only one layer, the segment count scales with neuron count, so you'd need 2^L neurons. For 10 layers: 20 versus 1,024.
