Deep Learning Intuition · 07 · Counts assume optimally placed kinks (= segments): a theoretical lower-limit guide.

Here's the idea behind the formula. Give every segment the same error allowance; its length h is then inversely proportional to √|f″|. Adding them up gives N ≈ B/√(8ε). Think of B as the curve's curvature budget: more bending, or more bends, means a larger B. x² and x⁵ are about equally hard. T₅ is also fifth-degree, but needs over three times as many neurons because it swings up and down four times. |x| needs just 2 neurons for an exact fit because it is already piecewise linear. These functions take values around −1 to 1, so ε = 0.01 is roughly a 1% error.
