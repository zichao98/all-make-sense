01 · One neuron

One ReLU neuron = one kink

One side is 0: where the input to ReLU is negative, the output is 0

One side is a line: its slope is w

Kink

b sets the position; w sets the slope and direction

The output weight a scales the kink up or down, or flips it upside down.

Deep Learning Intuition · 02

The animation on the right varies w and b. Watch the kink move with −b/w. Every few seconds, w changes sign and the sloping side switches over. The key idea: a ReLU neuron always draws just one kink. Its only special features are where that kink sits and how steep the line is.
