1D vs. higher dimensions

In 1D, one layer can make local shapes. In 2D and beyond, creases span the space; depth makes them local.

Deep Learning Intuition · 21

In 1D, two ReLUs can make a local tent, as with g on slide 11. In 2D, they can't: a finite sum of straight-crease functions cannot be nonzero only in a small patch unless everything cancels. Add a layer. First, 4 ReLUs build 1 − |x₁| − |x₂|, highest at the center and increasingly negative outside. A second ReLU cuts all negatives to 0, leaving a little pyramid confined to the middle. These local blocks let us build surfaces like the triangle meshes in 3D games. This is another important role for depth in higher dimensions.
