Picture a straight segment whose ends sit on the curve. The curve bows away in the middle. The gap depends on the segment length h and the curvature f''. Length enters as a square: twice as many segments means one quarter of the error. That's why the error of a 1D, one-hidden-layer ReLU network scales roughly like 1/N².
