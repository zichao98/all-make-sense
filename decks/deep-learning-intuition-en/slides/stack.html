Deep Learning Intuition · 03

When you add ReLUs, each neuron changes the slope at one location. So a ReLU network with one hidden layer and a 1D input always draws a piecewise linear curve; the neuron count is the kink count. Writing ReLU(x − kᵢ) simply absorbs the size of w into aᵢ. Notice that the kinks on the right are not evenly spaced. x⁵ is nearly flat in the middle and bends near the ends, so the kinks cluster there too.
