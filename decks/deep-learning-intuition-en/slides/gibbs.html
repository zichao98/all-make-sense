Deep Learning Intuition · 15

Fourier series assume periodicity. x⁵ is 1 at the right endpoint and −1 at the left. Repeating it creates a jump of height 2 at the seam. Ripples persist near the endpoints (the Gibbs phenomenon), and maximum error won't fall away; only average error gradually shrinks. ReLU can place its kinks where needed without assuming a periodic extension, so it avoids this problem. Conversely, Fourier fits a many-wave signal like sin(20πx) exactly with one term, while ReLU needs hundreds of neurons. Choose a basis that suits the shape.
