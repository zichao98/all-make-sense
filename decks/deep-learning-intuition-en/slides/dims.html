Part Five

Into two and three dimensions

A kink point becomes a crease line, then a crease plane.

Fourier changes too: a 1D wave becomes a plane wave with parallel crests in 2D. In higher dimensions, neurons and Fourier look even more alike.

So far, every input has been a single x. Real networks take many inputs: an image can have hundreds of thousands of pixels. Let's see what folding looks like in two and three dimensions, and what Fourier becomes there.
