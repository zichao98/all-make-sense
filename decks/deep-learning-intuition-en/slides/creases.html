14 · Many creases

Creases carve a plane into facets

N neurons = N creases. Within each region, every neuron is either on or off. The output is a flat facet, like a gemstone's surface.

Max regions in 2D

Max regions in d dimensions

But each crease cuts all the way across

A 1D kink sits at a point; a 2D crease spans the plane. One layer can't make a bump confined to a small area.

Deep Learning Intuition · 20

The right-hand view looks straight down at a one-layer network's output. Black lines are creases. Each color shows a facet's slope and direction. N lines can split a plane into at most 1 + N + N(N−1)/2 regions, roughly N²/2. In d dimensions, N hyperplanes give at most C(N,0) + C(N,1) + … + C(N,d) regions. That sounds like a lot, but you can't arrange the regions freely. Every crease is an infinitely long line cutting across the whole space. That makes isolated local detail hard with one layer, which leads to the next slide.
