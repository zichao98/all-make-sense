Deep Learning Intuition · 23

In 1D, Fourier uses sin(kx); in 2D, it uses sin(k₁x₁ + k₂x₂ + φ), a plane wave. Its crests are parallel straight lines, much like ReLU creases. Compare them: w sets a neuron's crease direction; k sets a wave's direction and frequency. b and φ both shift position. Both are ridge functions: they vary along one direction and stay constant perpendicular to it. Again, Fourier fixes k on an integer grid, while a neuron can learn w freely. Each 8×8 2D cosine basis pattern in JPEG has the form cos(ux)·cos(vy), which is a sum of two plane waves.
