Deep Learning Intuition · 08 · Counts assume ε = 0.01 and optimally placed kinks.

Back to the original question: how many neurons do I need for a fifth-degree function? Look at its shape, not its degree. x⁵ and x²⁰ are mostly flat with sharp turns near the ends; about 13–14 neurons reach 1% error. T₅ is also fifth-degree, but swings four times and needs 41. The eyeball rule comes from the previous table: x² has one bend and needs 10; sin(πx) has two and needs 17; T₅ has four and needs 41; sin(5πx) has ten and needs 85. That's roughly 6–10 neurons per bend.
