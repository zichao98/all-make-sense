Fourier: fixed input weights, sin activation. A neural network learns its shapes and positions.

Deep Learning Intuition · 14

A·sin(kπx + φ) is a neuron with sin activation: input weight w = kπ, bias b = φ, and output weight a = A. Structurally, a Fourier series is a one-hidden-layer network. But its w values are fixed at integer multiples of a frequency. Only A and φ need choosing, and integration can calculate them directly without training. A neural network also lets w and b move. This is an adaptive basis; the price is having to find it with gradient descent.
