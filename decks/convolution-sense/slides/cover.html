From the kinks of a ReLU to the kernels of a CNN

Convolution Sense

One small neuron, copied to every position.

Kinks · Kernels · Weight sharing · Padding · Stride · Receptive field

This deck answers one question: how does the idea of a kink, one ReLU neuron folding the input once, grow into convolution, the workhorse of image networks? The short answer is in the subtitle. A convolution is one small neuron, with a few weights, run at every position of the input. Along the way we use the counting rules from Dumoulin and Visin's “A guide to convolution arithmetic for deep learning” (2016) to predict output sizes. The animation shows the idea: a 3×3 window slides over the blue input, and each position produces one orange output cell.
