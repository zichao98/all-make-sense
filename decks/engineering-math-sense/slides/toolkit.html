Engineering Math Sense · 09　　Table adapted from the Capstone Lab article.

This is the article's toolkit table. Linear algebra represents data and transforms it: every layer, attention and embedding is a matrix operation. Calculus computes directions of change, which is what backpropagation and sensitivity analysis need. Probability and statistics describe uncertainty: classification outputs, cross-entropy, Bayesian methods, generative models and model evaluation. Numerical computing keeps answers reliable in finite precision: stable softmax, mixed-precision training, linear solves and eigenvalue routines. Optimization moves from an initial guess toward a better solution: SGD, Adam, Newton's method. Differential equations describe how systems evolve in time, and they reappear inside ResNets, Neural ODEs, diffusion models and state-space models.
