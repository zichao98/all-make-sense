What engineering math is doing inside deep learning

Engineering Math Sense

Four lines of training code. Seven branches of math.

Linear algebra · Calculus · Probability · Numerics · Optimization · ODEs · Fourier

This deck turns a short article, “高等工程数学与深度学习之间的关系” (The relationship between advanced engineering mathematics and deep learning) by Capstone Lab, into a set of intuitions you can use. The article's claim is simple: matrices, gradients, probability and iteration are not prerequisites you finish and forget. They are what the algorithm is actually doing. We keep its structure, a toolkit table and a symptom-to-math debugging index, and add a picture, a computed example and a rule of thumb for each branch. The animation is real gradient descent on a long, narrow valley. The path zig-zags across the steep direction and creeps along the flat one, which is the most common shape of a training problem. Every number in the deck was computed for it.
