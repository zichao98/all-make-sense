A neural network is new packaging on old structure

Samples, arrays and update loops are the same tools behind finite elements, signal processing and control.

Engineering Math Sense · 05

The three translations. First, discretize: a sound is a continuous pressure wave, but the computer stores samples. One second at 16 kHz is 16,000 numbers. Second, arrange as arrays: a colour image of 224 by 224 pixels with three channels is a tensor of 224 × 224 × 3 = 150,528 numbers, and a batch stacks many of them. Third, iterate: almost nothing in deep learning is solved in closed form. We start with a guess for the parameters θ and repeatedly move them a step η against the gradient of the loss. None of this is new. Finite-element solvers discretize space, signal processing works on sampled arrays, and control systems iterate. Deep learning reuses these structures at a very large scale.
